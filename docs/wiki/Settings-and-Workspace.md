# Settings & Workspace

## Top Navigation Bar

The sticky top navigation bar organizes your workflow into four primary sections:

1. **`Import Deck` (`Import`):** Paste lists, import deck URLs, upload files, or pick sample decks.
2. **`Deck Overview` (`Overview`):** Land/spell gauge, category targets, format audits, radar, and budget overview.
3. **`Mana, Changes & List` (`Mana & List`):** Turn-T mana calculator, deep metrics, synergy audit, card gallery, and optimized export.
4. **`Bot Playtest` (`Playtest`):** Monte Carlo simulations and Live Tabletop 1v1 / 4-Player Commander pods.

---

## My Decks & Profiles

Click **`My Decks`** in the top-right to open your saved deck workspace and profile controls:
- **Version History:** Save and restore snapshots of your deck as you test changes.
- **Local Profiles:** Create local profiles with optional passcodes to keep decks and bot matchup memory separated per user on a shared PC.
- **Optional Firebase Cloud Sync:** If you configure `.env` using `.env.example` and `firebase-applet-config.json`, you can optionally sign in to sync saved decks and bot memory across devices.

## Settings Menu (6 Tabs)

Open **Settings** in the top-right corner to access six streamlined sections:

| Tab | Contents |
| --- | --- |
| **General** | Open **AI Engine & Local Models**, reopen **Welcome & .gguf Setup Guide**, check **Software Updates & Rollback** (GitHub Releases), toggle startup welcome & release-notification preferences, and exit cleanly. |
| **About** | App overview, core capabilities summary, and creator/stack credits. |
| **Open Source** | Direct link and one-click copy for `https://github.com/bssman89/umbras-pass`, shortcuts to source tree, releases, and issues, plus why Umbras Pass is open source. |
| **Resources** | Quick links to **Card Marketplaces** (TCGplayer, Card Kingdom, CardTrader, eBay) and **Deck Platforms & Resources** (Arena, Moxfield, Archidekt, MTGGoldfish, ManaBox, MTGO, Cockatrice, Scryfall, Deckstats, TappedOut, CubeCobra, MTGTop8, Pastebin, EDHREC). |
| **Support** | Support email (`umbraspass.support@gmail.com`) with copy action, plus an in-app **Bug Report & Feedback** form. |
| **Disclaimers** | Full unofficial fan-project notice, trademark attribution, Scryfall data attribution, and "as is" liability notice. |
